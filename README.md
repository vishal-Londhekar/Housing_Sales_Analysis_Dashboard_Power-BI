🏠 Housing Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

📌 Project Overview -

This project focuses on analyzing housing market data to solve key business challenges in real estate sales and pricing. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, pricing behavior, and regional trends using Power BI.
The objective is to convert raw housing data into actionable insights that support strategic decision-making for stakeholders such as real estate companies, investors, and analysts.

🎯 Business Problem -

The real estate market generates large volumes of data, but decision-makers often struggle with:

•	Limited visibility into regional sales performance.
•	Difficulty understanding price variations across property types.
•	Lack of clarity on factors influencing property prices.
•	Inefficient tracking of sales trends over time.

✅ Solution Approach -

This project solves the above problems by:

•	Building an interactive Power BI dashboard
•	Creating KPI-driven insights for business users
•	Performing data analysis to identify trends and drivers
•	Enabling dynamic filtering by region, property type, and time

📈 Dashboard Features -

🔹 1. Market Overview -

•	Regional price change analysis

🔹 2. Sales Performance Analysis -
•	Sales distribution across regions

🔹 3. Price & Property Analysis -
•	Average price per square meter by region 
•	Offer-to-SQM ratio by sales type 
•	Property feature influence on pricing 
👉 The Key Influencers visual (Page 2) shows how property age impacts price significantly. 

🔹 4. Property Type Insights -
•	Comparison of Offer Price vs Purchase Price across property types 
•	Analysis of inflation, interest rate, and yield 
•	SQM vs SQM price comparison 
👉 As shown on Page 3, apartments have higher price per SQM, while farms have larger area sizes. 

🧠 Key Business Insights -
•	Zealand dominates the housing market in terms of total sales 
•	There is a strong correlation between offer price and purchase price 
•	Property features such as age significantly influence pricing 
•	Apartments tend to have higher price per square meter compared to other property types 
•	Sales types like regular sales generate higher value compared to auctions 

💡 Business Recommendations -
•	Focus investment and marketing strategies on high-performing regions like Zealand 
•	Optimize pricing strategies based on property features and type 
•	Monitor market trends using YoY growth for better forecasting 
•	Improve decision-making using data-driven insights from pricing and sales patterns
