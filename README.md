## 📌 Project Overview  

This project focuses on analyzing housing market data to solve key business challenges in real estate sales and pricing.  

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of **sales performance, pricing behavior, and regional trends** using Power BI.  

The objective is to convert raw data into **actionable insights** that support strategic decision-making for stakeholders such as real estate companies, investors, and analysts.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem  

The real estate market generates large volumes of data, but decision-makers often struggle with:

- Limited visibility into regional sales performance  
- Difficulty understanding price variations across property types  
- Lack of clarity on factors influencing property prices  
- Inefficient tracking of sales trends over time  

➡️ Result: **Inefficient decision-making and suboptimal pricing strategies**

---

## ✅ Solution Approach  

This project solves the above problems by:

- Building an interactive Power BI dashboard  
- Creating KPI-driven insights for business users  
- Performing data analysis to identify trends and drivers  
- Enabling dynamic filtering by region, property type, and time

## 📈 Dashboard Features  

### 🔹 Market Overview  
- Year-over-Year sales growth by sales type  
- Offer Price vs Purchase Price comparison  
- Regional price change analysis  

👉 Highlights overall market trends and pricing relationships  

---

### 🔹 Sales Performance Analysis  
- Sales distribution across regions  
- Time-based tracking (Year, Quarter, Month, Day)  
- YTD Sales tracking  

👉 Enables trend analysis and performance tracking  

---

### 🔹 Price & Property Analysis  
- Average price per SQM by region  
- Offer-to-SQM ratio by sales type  
- Property feature influence on pricing  

👉 Identifies key pricing drivers  

---

### 🔹 Property Type Insights  
- Offer vs Purchase price comparison  
- Inflation, interest rate, and yield analysis  
- Area vs price relationship  

👉 Compares performance across property types  

---

## 🧠 Key Business Insights  

- Zealand dominates the housing market in total sales  
- Strong correlation between offer price and purchase price  
- Property features (age, size) significantly impact pricing  
- Apartments have higher price per SQM  
- Regular sales generate higher value than other sales types  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations  

- Focus on high-performing regions like Zealand  
- Optimize pricing using property features  
- Use trends for forecasting demand  
- Improve decision-making using data insights
